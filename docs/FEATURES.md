# Feature Overview

## Customer

- Phone-based OTP sign-in
- Service selection
- Barber selection
- Date and time availability
- Individual bookings
- Group bookings
- Appointment confirmation
- Rescheduling
- Cancellation
- Mobile-responsive booking flow

## Barber

- Appointment schedule
- Working hours
- Break management
- Time-off management
- Availability controls
- Manual appointment workflows

## Administration

- Staff management
- Service assignment
- Appointment management
- Customer management
- Manual bookings
- Schedule configuration
- Breaks and days off
- Calendar-based operational view
- Statistics and reporting tools
- Permission-aware access

## Production Operations

- Database-level authorization
- Production deployment
- Error/debugging workflows
- Iterative releases based on real business use
- Backup and disaster-recovery procedures
- Git-based version control

## Product Roadmap

The platform is being developed toward a reusable white-label model that can support multiple independent businesses while sharing a common technical foundation.
